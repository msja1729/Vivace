1. Your pitch (learn this)

Vivace runs oral exams for a whole class. Students join a class and upload their work. The instructor schedules a viva and splits students between themselves and their TAs, who interview in parallel.

During the video interview, the AI works in the background: it watches for cheating on the camera and mic, transcribes both people, grades each answer against the student's own work, and suggests the next question. At the end it writes a report with evidence.

The AI advises; the human examiner decides. Next.js frontend, FastAPI backend, SQLite, MediaPipe and YOLO for vision, Deepgram for speech, Claude or Gemini for grading. Hosted on my laptop through Tailscale Funnel.
